Privacy Policy

Oxirgi yangilanish: 19.11.2026

Last updated: 19.11.2026

Bu siyosat Referee Review loyihasi va uning "Referee Review Uploader" dasturi (keyingi o'rinlarda — "dastur") qanday ma'lumotlardan foydalanishini tushuntiradi.

Dastur nima qiladi

Dastur — loyiha egasining shaxsiy kompyuterida ishlaydigan ichki vosita. U faqat egasining o'z YouTube kanaliga (va Instagram akkauntiga) ulanadi: tayyor videolarni joylaydi, kanal statistikasini o'qiydi va videolar ostidagi izohlarni egasi ko'rib chiqishi uchun yig'adi. Dastur boshqa foydalanuvchilarning akkauntlariga kirmaydi va ommaga taklif qilinmaydi.

YouTube API xizmatlari

Dastur YouTube API Services'dan foydalanadi. Dasturdan foydalanib, siz YouTube foydalanish shartlariga rozilik bildirasiz. Google ma'lumotlardan qanday foydalanishi Google maxfiylik siyosatida yozilgan.

Qanday ma'lumotlar ishlatiladi

  • Kanal egasining ma'lumotlari: OAuth ruxsat tokeni, kanal nomi, videolar ro'yxati va ko'rishlar statistikasi. Token faqat egasining kompyuteridagi yopiq sozlamalar faylida saqlanadi, hech kimga berilmaydi va faqat video joylash va statistika olish uchun ishlatiladi.
  • Tomoshabinlarning ochiq izohlari (bu funksiya yoqilganda): izoh matni, foydalanuvchi nomi va vaqti — faqat egasi javob yozishi yoki izohni moderatsiya qilishi uchun. Ular 30 kundan keyin o'chiriladi.
  • Saqlash muddati: YouTube API Services orqali olingan har qanday ma'lumot (jumladan, videolarimiz statistikasi) 30 kundan ortiq saqlanmaydi: shu muddatda yangilanadi yoki o'chiriladi.

Ma'lumotlar kimga beriladi

Hech kimga sotilmaydi va berilmaydi. Izoh matni javob loyihasini tayyorlash uchun Anthropic kompaniyasining Claude AI xizmatiga yuborilishi mumkin; reklama yoki boshqa maqsadlarda ishlatilmaydi.

Bu sayt

Bu sayt cookie, reklama yoki tahliliy kuzatuv vositalaridan foydalanmaydi. Til tanlovingiz faqat brauzeringizda saqlanadi.

Ruxsatni bekor qilish va o'chirish

Dasturga berilgan ruxsatni istalgan vaqtda Google akkaunt ruxsatlari sahifasida bekor qilish mumkin. Saqlangan ma'lumotlarni o'chirishni so'rash uchun bizga elektron pochta orqali yozing — 30 kun ichida o'chiriladi.

O'zgarishlar

Siyosat o'zgarsa, shu sahifa va yuqoridagi sana yangilanadi.

This policy explains what data the Referee Review project and its "Referee Review Uploader" application ("the app") use.

What the app does

The app is an internal tool that runs on the project owner's own computer. It connects only to the owner's own YouTube channel (and Instagram account): it uploads finished videos, reads channel statistics and collects comments under our videos so the owner can review them. It does not access other users' accounts and is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. How Google uses data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data is used

  • Channel owner's data: the OAuth access token, channel name, list of our videos and their view statistics. The token is stored only in a private settings file on the owner's computer, is never shared, and is used only to upload videos and read statistics.
  • Viewers' public comments (when this feature is enabled): comment text, user name and time — only so the owner can reply or moderate. They are deleted after 30 days.
  • Retention: any data obtained through YouTube API Services (including our own videos' statistics) is not kept longer than 30 days: it is refreshed or deleted within that period.

Sharing

Data is never sold or shared. Comment text may be sent to Anthropic's Claude AI service to draft a reply; it is not used for advertising or any other purpose.

This website

This website uses no cookies, advertising or analytics trackers. Your language choice is stored only in your browser.

Revoking access and deletion

Access granted to the app can be revoked at any time on the Google account permissions page. To request deletion of stored data, write to us by email; it is deleted within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the date above are updated.
