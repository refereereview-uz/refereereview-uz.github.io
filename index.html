Hakam qarorlari — o'sha yilgi qoidalar bilan

Refereeing decisions, judged by the Laws of the day

Referee Review — futboldagi bahsli hakamlik qarorlarini mustaqil tahlil qiluvchi loyiha. Har bir holatni o'yin bo'lgan kundagi IFAB futbol qoidalari, ochiq manbalar va mavjud dalillar asosida tekshiramiz va natijani qisqa video sifatida o'zbek va ingliz tillarida e'lon qilamiz.

Referee Review is an independent project that analyses controversial refereeing decisions in football. Each incident is checked against the IFAB Laws of the Game in force on the match date, public sources and the available evidence, and the result is published as a short video in Uzbek and English.

Qanday ishlaymizHow we work

1. O'sha yilgi qoida

1. The Law of the day

2009 yilgi o'yin 2009 yilgi qoida bilan baholanadi, bugungi VAR protokoli bilan emas. Qoida bandi va sahifasi ko'rsatiladi.

A 2009 match is judged by the 2009 Laws, not by today's VAR protocol. The Law, paragraph and page are cited.

2. Dalil va manba

2. Evidence and sources

Har bir fakt manbasi bilan: rasmiy bayonotlar, hakamlik organlari, ishonchli OAV. Dalil yetmasa — to'qimaymiz, nima yetishmasligini aytamiz.

Every fact carries its source: official statements, referee bodies, reliable media. Where evidence is missing we say so instead of guessing.

4. 3D rekonstruksiya

4. 3D reconstruction

Videolarda translyatsiya lavhalari emas, o'zimiz yasagan 3D sxemalar ishlatiladi. Ovoz sun'iy intellekt yordamida yaratiladi; tahlilni AI agent tayyorlaydi va har bir hukmni loyiha muallifi tasdiqlaydi.

Videos use our own 3D schematics, not broadcast footage. Narration is AI-generated; the analysis is prepared by an AI agent and every verdict is approved by the project owner before publishing.

Bizning hukmlarimiz — mustaqil tahlil. Ular FIFA, UEFA, IFAB yoki biror liganing rasmiy qarori emas va ular bilan bog'liq emasmiz. Biz qarorlarni tanqid qilamiz, odamlarni emas.

Our verdicts are independent analysis. They are not official rulings of FIFA, UEFA, IFAB or any league, and we are not affiliated with them. We criticise decisions, not people.
